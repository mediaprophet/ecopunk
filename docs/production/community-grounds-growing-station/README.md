## Scoped target

Produce an editable, source-linked synthetic **modular growing station** derived from the existing Vibe source. Its authored concept contains a braced frame, six raised hydroponic channels on three levels per side, opening/lid and collar details, visual feed/return runs, and a sump/pump-cabinet representation. The nominal source frame dimensions are 2.60 m × 1.65 m × 1.72 m. The source intends approximately 199 identified part records, with 36 openings/collars. Batch 6 measured 199 identified parts, 9 materials, 199 ranges, 239,952 compiled vertices and 79,984 triangles. These are exact results for one film-intent fixture run, not engineering dimensions, complete topology, quality acceptance, or full-package qualification.

This is **not** a real installation design. Water-flow continuity, treatment, capacity, water quality, pump performance, power demand, electrical protection/isolation, structural loads, service life, hygiene, growing suitability, safety, compliance, and maintainability are unverified. Do not present this asset as suitable for construction or operation.

Plants are living species instances supplied by separately qualified species packages. They are not manufactured station parts, and no species, seedling, growth state, crop capacity, yield, or horticultural suitability is asserted here. Site placement and any plant-to-station relations require their own sourced identifiers, place/time data, and evidence.
