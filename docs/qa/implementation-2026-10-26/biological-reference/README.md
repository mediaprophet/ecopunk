# Kosciuszko biological reference work

Timothy selected **Kosciuszko National Park / Snowy Mountains, NSW** on
2026-10-26. These references extend the engineering rack trial to the biological
requirements already allocated by ECO-046/048/056/061–069/081–087. They do not
waive the native foundation gates or release asset production from HOLD.

The working subjects are **mountain white gum, Eucalyptus dalrympleana subsp.
dalrympleana**, and **swamp wallaby, Wallabia bicolor**. This is a reference scope,
not identification of a photographed individual or proof of a shared microsite.
Mountain white gum must not silently become alpine snow gum. The latter needs a
separate taxon concept and developmental/reference inventory if added later.

## Source-backed scope

- [EUCLID mountain white gum](https://apps.lucidcentral.org/euclid/text/entities/eucalyptus_dalrympleana_subsp._dalrympleana.htm)
  supplies the common-name mapping and diagnostic morphology. Its juvenile
  foliage and mature foliage differ; a uniformly scaled mature tree cannot
  satisfy the requested development sequence.
- [NSW Flora mountain gum](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=sp&name=Eucalyptus~dalrympleana&page=nswfl)
  provides a separate attributed description. Conflicting ranges and taxon
  treatments remain attributed rather than averaged into invented measurements.
- [NSW park plan](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/kosciuszko-national-park-plan-of-management-210174.pdf),
  printed pages 66 and 71, records regional mountain-gum forests and wallaby
  habitat distinctions. Regional occurrence does not imply every elevation,
  habitat or individual matches the same appearance.
- [NPWS swamp wallaby](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/swamp-wallaby)
  supplies the working animal name, regional occurrence and initial coat/habitat
  references. It does not supply a reviewed rig, motion capture or growth curve.

These are reference links and short attributed summaries. No source photographs,
recordings or textures have been copied or licensed for redistribution here.
Exact reference specimens, usable media rights, dimensions and biological review
remain work. No actual growth ages or forces have been inferred.
