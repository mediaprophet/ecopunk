# White gum tree cases — proposed flora coverage

**Status:** proposed acceptance coverage, 2026-10-26. The requested locality is
Kosciuszko National Park / Snowy Mountains, NSW; the exact site and individual
reference remain pending. The leading taxon scope is *Eucalyptus dalrympleana*
subsp. *dalrympleana* (mountain white gum), based on the user's clarification
and the cited regional references. Treat this as a sourced working focus pending
site-level identification and review, not an identified model or occurrence.
Do not silently substitute *Eucalyptus viminalis* or alpine snowgum
*E. niphophila*. Preserve vernacular-name mappings, taxon concept, source corpus,
rights and reviewer. Do not infer ages or unsourced dimensions, phenology, or
traits from this case list.

## Evidence boundary and use

This is proposed coverage, not a model or a site-level observation. The
diagnostic checks below are grounded in the linked EUCLID and PlantNET accounts;
their taxonomic descriptions are reference evidence, not observations of a
selected Kosciuszko individual. The remaining case structure names questions and
acceptance behavior only. Record each eventual assertion as
observed, sourced, modeled, inferred, generated, illustrative, unknown, or
unassessed, with its source, scope, time/context, uncertainty, rights, and review.
Do not turn a procedural choice or visual convention into a biological fact.
Stage labels below are inventory prompts, not asserted taxon stages or age bands;
replace or qualify them from reviewed evidence and preserve unknowns.

Expand these cases into the normal version-pinned dossier/claim ledger only when
a subject and locality are resolved. Retain all catalogue rows and expand
applicable parts, variants, movements, behaviours, profiles, and scenarios. This
file is not a claim ledger and its presence passes no catalogue item.
