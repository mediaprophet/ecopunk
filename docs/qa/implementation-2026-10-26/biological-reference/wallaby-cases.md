## Scope and evidence boundary

These are proposed acceptance cases, not observations or biological findings. The NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service species page reports swamp wallaby occurrences in the Kosciuszko region and describes a dark grey coat, light cheek stripe, long narrow feet, habitat/foraging and night activity ([NPWS species reference](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/swamp-wallaby)). The Kosciuszko National Park plan of management, p. 71, identifies swamp wallaby as present in the subalpine zone year-round ([park plan PDF](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/kosciuszko-national-park-plan-of-management-210174.pdf)). These are scoped regional references: they do not establish every microsite, a particular individual, exact measurements, or a legal taxon determination. Exact specimens/references and their permissions remain pending. Every biological assertion, dimension, range, behavior, habitat association, life-stage distinction, and visual reference must be sourced to the selected taxon concept and relevant locality/population, with provenance, applicability, uncertainty, and rights. Do not fill gaps from memory or generic “wallaby” conventions. Preserve conflicting sources and unknown values as such. No measured values or thresholds are prescribed here.

The eventual dossier must pin the template catalogue version/digest, retain all catalogue rows, and expand these stable IDs into claim records with applicability, status/result, exact source/artifact identifiers, evidence, review, and remaining work. No N/A without an applicable rule or authorized decision. Film, open-world game, and cartoon are distinct required targets; none is accepted by implication from another.
