**Status:** documentation/research only · **Source:** `docs/Biosphere algorithms Specifications.md`, read in full (547 lines) · **Scope:** 18 initial evidence files plus 5 bounded follow-up reads (23 total), including six saved source-review artifacts (not newly audited source code); no new source-body audit. ## Disposition

Keep the draft as a useful research/concept inventory, not an authoritative ontology, released format, implemented capability map, adopted rights instrument, or scientific validation. Its strongest contributions are the intended connection between Q42 knowledge and typed resources; complete HMC versus selectively admitted working sets; explicit organism/part/resource lineage; cross-scale representations; domain vocabularies; and inspectable source-to-output workflows. Those fit existing Ecopunk contracts when names and algorithms are reconciled with owners.

Several statements currently read as already implemented or guaranteed. The prior nine-location QualiaDB audit is explicitly targeted, not comprehensive (`docs/qa/implementation-2026-10-26/biosphere-integration-review/README.md:3-6,20-28`). It found selected useful owners and important limits, not proof of every subsystem named in this draft. Convert unreviewed claims to proposals/hypotheses and route concrete work through existing identifiers and packets.
