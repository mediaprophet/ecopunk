## Reconciliation and remaining conflicts

The 7.1 rules (`612-631`) properly say to keep authored/acquired and generated representations together, preserve existing formats/unknown sections, treat physical/cue states distinctly, and make frontier research optional. They substantially align with the prior corrections BR-01–16. For the bounded workflow, the operative additions are editable-source retention, reference/source context, explicit unknowns, and same-Poet authoring; the rest are future case-specific research.

The authority wording is still too strong in places. Section 4.1.1 calls “Inside-Out Compositional Constituency” a universal/foundational engine principle, asserts coupled anatomy/physics and says the approach applies universally (`397-437`). Its organ, tissue, mass/inertia, contact, optical perfusion, and five-tier/64-byte proxy claims are not qualified evidence here. Treat these as proposed, domain-reviewed hypotheses, not current Poet behavior, guaranteed physical response, or medical/surgical capability. The extension across organisms, terrain and planets (`421-430`) likewise does not establish one common implemented mechanism. No medical or physical guarantee follows from the “Medical Loupe” label (`435`).

The PU0–PU5 text repeatedly says the architecture “enforces” safeguards and that every model/rule/shard “must declare” PU in Q42/HMC metadata (`555-567`). It also asserts explicit causal graph semantics and hybrid process facilities (`571-603`). The prior review did not establish these as current fields or runtime gates. Recast them as proposed evidence policy; define ownership, semantics, versioning, and workflow before any mandatory storage or enforcement. In particular, a digest establishes byte identity/integrity under its assumptions, not accuracy; conservation constraints alone do not prove causal identification; and requirements such as intervention validation, fault injection, clade-blocked splits, prospective validation, and adaptive-model separation need their relevant data and domain review. The new empirical and clinical/bioprocess claims are unassessed in this task.

Two concrete technical wording issues remain:

1. **Weibull sign convention:** v2 correctly adds `ln(2)` to the earlier curve, but its prior definition says `P50` is negative (`-4.5`, `-1.5 MPa`; original `341`) while its corrected expression is `exp(-ln(2) × (-Psi/P50)^a)` (`v2 658-661`). At `Psi=P50<0`, the inner ratio is negative, so a general fractional exponent is undefined and the expression does not clearly give the intended half-conductivity. Specify whether `P50` stores a positive magnitude or a negative potential and use a sign-consistent normalized ratio. This is an algebra/definition correction only; do not tune or claim biological calibration here.
2. **Differential-growth repulsion:** the original surface equation assigns an explicit `F_repel` term and attributes it to `differential_growth.rs` (`original 352-355`). v2 describes the existing body as a “spring/repulsion constraint” kernel (`v2 662-664`), while prior BR-08 establishes rest-edge growth/constraint corrections and local splits, not the asserted surface cotangent/reaction-diffusion implementation (`review 63`). Keep the useful rest-edge kernel and local splits; label the repulsion-force attribution as unverified by that bounded review unless its owning body is separately checked. v2's proposed distinct surface solver/shader must remain a proposal, not a current capability.

There is also a **precedence ambiguity**: BR-01–16 are called mandatory corrections (`v2 633-635`), but the PU section contains new universal mandatory metadata (`558`) while BR-03 and the reconciliation preserve current binary contracts and require versioned extensions (`645-647`, `627-629`). Clarify that BR corrections govern factual distinctions and compatibility, while PU storage/enforcement and new internal-constituency layouts are optional, separately versioned proposals. Do not infer an automatic ABI, gate, or schema from this prose.
