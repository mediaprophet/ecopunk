## 2. Intended useful outcome

Timothy's objective is to produce exceptionally high-quality Ecopunk assets and places through his existing native technology stack. Assets must be editable, knowledge-bearing and grounded in relevant evidence, rather than merely attractive meshes or labelled demonstrations. They must support authoring, query, inference, simulation, inspection, presentation and reviewed downstream use.

Quality is assessed against declared visual, anatomical, geographic, physical, usability and resource criteria for the particular case. Claims of a “superior” product require actual comparisons and accepted results; additional code, more polygons, library inventories and source-file hashes do not establish superiority.

Complete embodiments retain their semantic corpus, editable sources, resources, identifiers, provenance and derivation lineage. Complete species/thing packages require film, open-world-game and cartoon representations, with additional scientific or educational profiles where applicable. Game-demo consumes explicit reviewed derivatives for people-led civics scenarios; its device budget does not set the master-quality ceiling.

## 3. Full scope of works

The programme covers the living Earth and its coupled environment. A botanical case is a starting production case, not a flora-only restriction on Ecopunk or permissible improvements.

| Scope | Required coverage for selected production cases |
|---|---|
| Plants | Roots, stems/branches, leaves and reproductive structures; connected surfaces, development, variation, materials and relevant environmental responses. Other plant forms require their own morphology rather than a universal tree grammar. |
| Vertebrates | Coherent anatomy, skin/fur/feathers/scales, rigs and deformation, locomotion/contact, behaviour, sound and life stages. |
| Invertebrates | Relevant segmentation, appendages, joints, exoskeleton/soft-body distinctions, wings/optics, development and terrestrial/aquatic interactions. |
| Funga and microorganisms | Hyphae/networks, fruiting structures and substrate relationships; cells/colonies/biofilms, calibrated scales and supported microscopy/volume representations as applicable. |
| Habitats and ecological processes | Organisms, populations and communities; trophic/symbiotic relationships, dispersal, decomposition, disturbance and recovery, with individual and population processes distinguished. |
| Terrain, geography, geology and soils | Source-backed places, coordinate/datum integrity, landforms, strata and material properties, weathering/erosion and surface/subsurface interfaces. |
| Water and atmosphere | Freshwater, wetlands, groundwater, coasts/oceans, weather and relevant climate context; sourced fields, time, units and declared physical approximations. |
| Space weather | Solar activity, solar wind/interplanetary magnetic field, geomagnetic and ionospheric conditions, aurora and applicable radiation/electrical effects. Solar/lunar positions alone do not cover these dynamics. Any ecological causal coupling requires its own evidence and validity assessment. |
| Related applications | People, made-world systems, comparative anatomy/scientific education, recognisable places and understandable civics scenarios, under their existing contracts. |

Coverage is extended through declared cases with explicit outstanding obligations. This does not claim universal biological simulation, clinical validity, complete classification coverage or completion of every domain before useful first assets can be authored.

## 4. Governing architecture and ownership requirements

1. **QualiaDB is the core runtime and semantic/data owner.** Reuse its native graph, NQuins/Q42, identifiers, logic systems, domain operations, solvers, codecs, provenance and authority mechanisms. Do not substitute a private database or parallel runtime.
2. **Poet is the unified human authoring interface.** Preserve and extend the intended Tool-Chest/Manifolds experience and useful existing project, source, knowledge and spatial services. Craft-origin capabilities become internal functionality with required notices retained.
3. **Etalon is the developed GPU fork.** Claims must identify the actually resolved revision, features, caller, backend and device. A capability in local source is not proof that a delivered frame uses it.
4. **Aether/Webizen and Chora retain their established boundaries.** Aether is the controlled application framework; Webizen Desktop supplies launcher/host services. Chora is a separate engine with a separate source/licensing boundary. Framework migration must preserve product behavior.
5. **Native and WASM qualification are distinct.** The native product must not require JavaScript, Node or V8. Supported WASM projections share semantics and declare any authorized native-provider dependency. Browser execution cannot establish native qualification.
6. **VibeScript is the authored orchestration/application language.** Reuse existing native operations through shared authoritative host routes; distinguish language-version proposals, released compatibility and missing bindings.
7. **Repository modularisation preserves the product.** The user's separation of a former monorepo into repositories was intended to modularise existing capability and improve development/build overhead. It was not permission to replace the product. Build improvements require measurement.

## 5. Data, ontology, inference and computation requirements

The growing Q42 corpus includes source schemas/ontologies, instance/observation assertions, relationships, methods and derived conclusions. Sources must retain their namespace and provenance. Existing RDF IRIs and term semantics are preserved. Where a provider supplies CSV, JSON or another non-RDF structure, its dataset/schema and records receive source-qualified semantic mappings and identifiers, with original fields/keys retained. A domain or matching name alone does not establish referent identity or equivalent meaning.

The corpus must be usable by existing QualiaDB symbolic logic systems. The selected engine/API, rules, premises, mappings, context, limits and available explanation must be inspectable. Validation, logical entailment, numerical estimation and model-generated suggestions are different operations. Counts or matches must not be represented as a proof or a complete inference closure that was not returned.

When a value is invalid or absent, the user requested a graph function to derive a suitable estimate from other collected comparable observations. The method must be declared and user-selectable or user-defined. Median is one option; there is no compulsory universal estimator. Preserve the invalid source value and reason, then record any estimate separately with its cohort/query, method revision, parameters, units, time/taxon/state/locality context, uncertainty, exclusions and actual execution status. Inadequate comparable evidence leaves the value unresolved. Changes to a source or method must identify affected conclusions, asset parameters and review decisions.

Users may define methods using private, proprietary or classified information they are authorized to access. Input selection, execution, explanations, logs, caches, derived results and exports must respect the applicable existing rights and access policies. An aggregate is not automatically cleared for disclosure. The documentation does not certify a classified-computing deployment, require confidential inputs to be uploaded or grant authority to disclose them.

These requirements are specified in the [corpus and inference chapter](../../../planning/whole-biosphere-implementation/01-corpus-and-inference.md) and [source namespace contract](../../../planning/tech/evidence-led-asset-production.md#7-source-namespaces-and-native-symbolic-inference).
