## Cross-cutting presentation and acceptance

- Native Poet must be usable as one product, including selected document/context, discoverable menus/shortcuts, viewport/canvas/timeline, direct manipulation, inspectable status, accessibility, resource and service lifetimes. Preserve original useful workflow semantics, not a collection of independent Craft applications. A screen, API, source file or placeholder is not the contributor/reviewer source-to-output evidence required by IP-03 C1–C8.
- Native Aether/Etalon/Webizen host path must be qualified for actual renderer/device provenance, no required JS/Node/V8 application workflow, input/resize/accessibility, launcher app launch/focus, service lifecycle and recovery. ENV-01–07 and REQ-HOST-001–011 remain specific cases; WebView desktop is not proof of native renderer use.
- WASM-local and WASM/native-assisted projections share versioned authored semantics and one state owner; declare browser binding dependencies and per-operation native providers separately. Execute absent/incompatible/unauthorized/revoked provider, disconnect/reconnect, cancel, stale and no-duplicate-effect cases. Native-only fixture success does not qualify WASM.
- For every domain preserve capability availability, original and editable source, typed identifiers and qualified mappings, document revision/history, relevant units/times/colour, provenance, contribution/rights/authority, package/reopen, actual output and required human decisions. Subject/part/occurrence and local keys are not interchangeable. Honest unsupported/held states preserve work.
- Keep Q42/graph/knowledge, simulator state, authoring session, render projection and HMC resource closure with their established owners. Before allocating a missing domain primitive, trace applicable domains, modalities, solvers, specialized_libs, wgsl_forge, qualia-vision, qualia-audio, webizen-render and semantic_instruments, plus qualia-sim, including public owner, actual host and test/receipt. This pass relies on cited bounded reviews and does not perform that full operation-by-operation audit.
- A complete species/thing is independently assessed for film, open-world game and cartoon; preserve catalogue/expanded inventories and per-deliverable evidence, editable HMC resources, domain/visual reviews and unresolved IDs. This authoring audit is not a model dossier or an installed CI gate. Asset production remains HOLD under the saved project direction.
