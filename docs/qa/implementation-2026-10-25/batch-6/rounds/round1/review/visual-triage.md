## Prioritized findings

1. **P1 — Cabinet inspection is insufficient.** Observed: `cabinet-closeup.png` cuts off the cabinet's left and lower extents, devotes roughly the upper third to an intervening channel, and shows mostly an unarticulated green face plus a projecting pipe. It does not make the hatch, handle, hinges, vents or service plaque assessable. The hero view also leaves the small cabinet partly hidden behind channels. Inference: camera placement/view direction and occlusion are immediate contributors; the image alone cannot distinguish hidden, reversed or misplaced cabinet details. Owner/remedy: reference shot authoring first. Reuse the existing `PreviewCamera` target/yaw/pitch/distance controls in `native/examples/growing_reference/render.rs` to frame the full cabinet from its service side and add a detail view showing the hatch perimeter, handle and vents together. Compare those identified parts' transforms against the accepted source before concluding they are absent. No camera-engine gap is established.

2. **P1 — Circular collars do not close the surrounding lid apertures.** Observed: `channel-closeup.png` shows repeated rectangular exposed corners surrounding the circular dark collars; the cover reads as separate lid strips with square slots rather than a continuous panel fitted to each opening. This recurs across the two central channels. Inference supported by source inspection: the accepted source assembles rectangular `g_lid_edge`/`g_lid_bay` pieces around gaps and uses a torus collar (`g_collar`), leaving rectangular corners beyond its footprint. This is a composition/design issue unless a later isolated geometry check shows otherwise. Owner/remedy: editable master/lid construction. Rebuild the aperture surround to meet the collar with an explicit flange or shaped infill, preserving the existing part identifiers and clear channel opening. Existing rounded/polyline profile sweeps and torus operators can author that surround; investigate them before requesting a boolean-cutting capability. Re-render the same closeup to verify that no unintended corner voids remain. No engine gap is established by this image.

3. **P2 — Curved-surface shading and highlights need refinement.** Observed: the collars in `channel-closeup.png` have visibly segmented highlight contours; the hero's metal cross-braces contain narrow almost-white streaks that overwhelm their otherwise slender shapes. Together with the largely uniform frame/cabinet surfaces, the render reads as a technical assembly preview and does not yet demonstrate convincing finished material treatment. Inference: tessellation/normal policy and material/light choices are plausible contributors, not isolated proven causes. The retained source uses 48 ring segments/12 tube segments, factor-only materials, and a highly metallic brace material. The spatial transport currently projects vertices/triangles without an authored normal stream; assembly has an existing flat-face fallback and also supports supplied normals. Owner/remedy: Poet spatial projection/master surface authoring plus reference lighting. First compare a curved part with existing authored-normal support and controlled segment changes; separately inspect roughness and exposure under the existing render settings. Retain geometry bounds and resource accounting. The renderer already accepts frame/material streams, so these images do not prove a missing rendering capability. Texture/microdetail or broader shading requirements should be allocated only after this narrower comparison identifies what remains.
