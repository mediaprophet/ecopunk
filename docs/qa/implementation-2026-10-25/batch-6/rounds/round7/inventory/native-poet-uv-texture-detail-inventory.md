# Round 7 inventory — native Poet UV unwrap, atlas, texture and detail authoring

**Date:** 2026-10-25. **Scope:** bounded read-only inspection of live sources in `C:/github/qualiaDB/crates`, `C:/github/poet`, `C:/github/craft`, and relevant `poet-artcraft`, `poet-photocraft`, and `poet-lightcraft` directories. No sibling source was changed. No Cargo/build/test/GUI execution was performed. This is source/API presence evidence only; assets remain HOLD.

## Handoff summary

Round 6 already added native Poet controls for stored UV0, part-local XY/XZ/YZ planar projection, and reversible UV0 affine transforms; texture resources can be imported through the project-owned `imports` library, bound as immutable digest-checked HMC snapshots, assigned to typed material map roles, and compiled into source/HMC. Core also has deterministic procedural surface-map generation. The boundary is explicit: **there is no general mesh UV unwrap/charting/packing, authored atlas construction, or mesh UV-space texture/detail painting demonstrated or wired in native Poet.**

The closest existing painting implementation is Photocraft's separate 2D raster brush engine. Its public surface is `Stroke`/`StrokePoint`, `StrokeRenderer`, `BrushContext`, and `render_stroke`; it operates on raster `Surface` coordinates and does not establish a 3D mesh/UV atlas editor, HMC material-resource binding, or Poet integration. Reuse may be possible at the brush/dab level, but an owner adapter, UV-space canvas mapping, resource lifecycle, source/revision persistence, and native Poet surface remain missing/unqualified.
