## Submission and review criteria

The producers should send the following for independent review. These are review criteria, not claims that a native gate already enforces them.

1. **Referent and evidence.** Taxon/concept source, scoped identifiers, synthetic/individual distinction, intended state/sex/life stage where applicable, rights/source records, diagnostic constraints and explicit unsupported artistic choices. Evidence references must identify source/version and the claim they support. A label or regional occurrence does not identify a specimen.
2. **Editable candidate.** Native authoring source and parameter/rule-to-component mapping; stable part identifiers; full source and dependency pins; resource budget and expected refusal behavior. Reuse existing geometry, render, rig and package owners. The source must be inspectable, not merely a screenshot or opaque mesh.
3. **Comparable visible outputs.** Exact source/output revision; camera, scale, light and material settings; whole subject front/side/three-quarter views; diagnostic closeups; neutral silhouette; contextual scale view. Before/after uses matched conditions. A view not yet produced remains pending, never marked reviewed.
4. **White-gum comparisons.** Whole-tree architecture, branch attachment/taper/junctions, leaf silhouette/orientation/attachment, bark treatment and required diagnostic reproductive/developmental organs. The exact set and tolerances derive from the chosen evidence and purpose, not an invented universal score. A branch study is explicitly partial relative to the whole-tree package.
5. **Wallaby comparisons.** Body/head/ear/muzzle proportions and coat boundaries, hands/feet and tail shape/contact, limb pose and deformation. Movement review needs transitions and ground-contact footage for the declared supported actions; a rest pose cannot close hopping/feeding/turning or the complete behavior inventory. Scientific/internal views require separately sourced anatomy review.
6. **Native checks.** Meaningful bounds/finite values/index/stream integrity, source-to-output reproducibility, package entry reconciliation and exact executed logs. Named tests must match the selected source revision. Negative cases and failures remain visible. A successful compile or vertex count is not an appearance criterion.
7. **Product route.** Identify standalone candidate preview, native Poet editing/review and final demo/consumer integration separately. Required preview/undo/save/fresh-reopen and contributor/reviewer behavior need actual execution evidence. A CLI/native helper alone cannot close IP-03 C1–C8.
8. **Claims and review.** Retain the pinned full catalogue, explicit inventories/expanded cases, all remaining IDs and three target statuses. Producer self-check, independent coordinator findings, competent domain review where required, and Timothy's decision are separately attributed. No empty inventory may vacuously pass.
