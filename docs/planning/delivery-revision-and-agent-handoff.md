## Product direction, retained from the original engagement

Ecopunk must produce exceptional editable flora, fauna, funga, Earth-system,
made-world and place embodiments. Complete species/thing knowledge and resources
travel in HMC, with Q42 as the semantic spine. Reviewed derivatives serve
`C:/github/game-demo` and people-led **civics** experiences related to dev.civics.au.
Consumer limits cannot lower the master-quality ceiling. The local white-gum and
wallaby references are the next biological capability cases, not the entire
product or a waiver of the wider asset/place/scenario inventory.

QualiaDB and its custom dependencies are part of the intended product. Reuse and
advance their actual geometry, physics, logic, data, spectral, media and rendering
owners. Native authoring/query/simulation/presentation requires no JS/Node/V8.
Poet is the unified interface; MIT-licensed source capabilities become internal
Poet functionality while required notices and existing useful behaviour survive.
Aether supports the native Webizen launcher/host and harmonised WASM projections;
Etalon is the developed GPU fork; Chora retains its separate code/licence boundary.

Keep Vibe 0.1 compatibility and the proposed 0.2 migration distinct. Use multiple
qualified **identifiers**, not a single universal record key. Retain cooperative
authority, authorship, reviews, unpaid contribution and source-rights records from
the start. Commercial obligation recovery, human-rights no-charge treatment and
provider-controlled funded allocations remain as specified; live payment rails
are later support work. Do not invent adoption, consent, review, payment or discharge.

Complete packages require the pinned full model catalogue and expanded cases,
with film, open-world-game and cartoon assessed separately. Anatomy/scientific
education, whole/part/developmental representations, physically typed EMF/cues and
query/magnifier-selected streaming add explicit evidence requirements; they are
not satisfied by a still image, generic mesh, opaque canopy or a species label.

## Copyable assignment and acceptance record

To assign another agent, copy the register's `assignment_prompt` for the chosen
job. The agent's two outputs must let a fresh agent continue without this chat:

1. **Purpose and authority:** job, original product outcome and IP/ECO/G/BSA/BGS
   requirements; current research/implementation scope and exact write boundary.
2. **Inputs and reuse:** absolute paths, examined symbols/ranges, SHA256 pins,
   public owner/caller, relevant source tests and prior command receipts. State
   what was searched, read, executed or not established.
3. **Result and next work:** concrete proposed contract/findings, unresolved
   decisions, dependency and exact re-entry condition; new capability versus
   missing binding/repair/qualification clearly classified.
4. **Evidence levels:** source-present, applied, compiled, executed, rendered,
   independently reviewed, domain-reviewed and human-accepted separately. Pending
   is not pass; N/A needs an applicable rule/reason, not convenience.
5. **Process continuity:** command/PID/session/owner/start/log/working directory
   and running/exit/unknown state, or explicit `none` for read-only work. Verify
   another agent can resume a live command before transferring ownership.
6. **Review surface:** concise findings document, machine handoff, exact artifact
   links/hashes, remaining IDs and the next bounded action. No claim that an
   agent-generated receipt or review constitutes a human act.

The coordinator checks handoffs against source and requirements, integrates
documentation centrally and records discrepancies. Future implementation adds
reviewed patches and actual checks; it does not overwrite prior failures or
claim the complete model from the smallest successful demonstration.
