## Visual review protocol — V01–V03

These are prepared review instructions for IP-04 and later production, after the relevant foundation gates and explicit production authorisation for the selected scope. They are **not new prerequisites requiring completed species masters before the C1–C8 tooling trial**. Existing diagnostic outputs can inform the criteria now. The later civics experience review retains its original implementation phase; it does not make a completed game-demo a prerequisite for authoring the first master.

Use identified, rights-appropriate references and the same declared referent/state for comparisons. Record camera projection, framing, scale, exposure, lighting, output resolution, renderer/profile and source digest. Use a neutral diagnostic lighting setup as well as the intended scene lighting; neither should conceal defects. Choose actual values in the brief and retain them with outputs. No numeric quality or performance threshold is established by this packet.

| Inspection | Required review / failure examples | Existing trace |
|---|---|---|
| Whole form and composition | Compare diagnostic silhouette, proportions, component architecture and ground/context contact against the selected evidence. Uniformly scaling one mesh is not proof of developmental variation. | TREE-03/04/10/11; WALLABY-04/05/08/10; IP-04 |
| Part and close study | Inspect applicable branch junctions, leaf attachment/forms, bark/reproductive structures, anatomy/coat, surface variation and semantic part selection. Record seams, repeated motifs, missing structures and unsourced detail. An attractive texture cannot establish correct anatomy. | TREE-05/06/10; WALLABY-04/06/07; catalogue KN/geometry/surface obligations |
| Motion and contact | Inspect applicable wind, pose/clip changes, deformation, attachment, normals/tangents, UV/material continuity and foot/tail/body/ground contacts. Look for popping, tearing, penetration, sliding and implausible transitions; distinguish simulation assumptions from observations. | TREE-08/11; WALLABY-07/09; source/rig/behavior inventories |
| Three profiles | Review film, open-world game and cartoon separately. Keep style, LOD/residency, biological state, simulation fidelity and target distinct. Record derivation and measured losses. Delivery budgets cannot lower master criteria. | TREE-12; WALLABY-12; catalogue ID-04 and profile cases |
| Knowledge and place | Select a named part/state and retrieve its qualified identifiers, evidence and available representations. Inspect declared locality/context without implying an exact observation from regional material. | TREE-01/02/09/13; WALLABY-01/02/10/13; REQ-SP-001–008 |
| Civics purpose | For an agreed scenario, ask actual participants what problem, opportunity, alternatives and consequences they understood; preserve uncertainty and distinguish people-led civics from government civic activity. No comprehension score is fabricated. | WALLABY-11; high-grade production/civics contract |

Record a failure with the actual output and reference, source/settings, suspected versus demonstrated cause, owning layer, concrete remedy and retest case. Keep source quality, tooling/host wiring, rendering, data/reference availability and scientific uncertainty separate. Do not assign the renderer blame without evidence or dismiss weak output as inevitable.

V01 is the fixed-view whole/part/close-study review; V02 is motion/contact/continuity; V03 is profile/knowledge/civics continuity. They support existing requirements and do not replace per-model expanded claims.
