## Shared authoring and evidence contract

1. **Reuse first.** Preserve saved botanical turtle admission/execution, the earlier wallaby rig/stepping/preview repairs, native graph/format/Q42 owners, and relevant domain source. The current [nine-location review index](../../qa/implementation-2026-10-26/biosphere-integration-review/README.md) covers selected bodies across `domains`, `modalities`, `solvers`, `specialized_libs`, `wgsl_forge`, `qualia-vision`, `qualia-audio`, `webizen-render`, and `semantic_instruments`; it is a bounded review, not blanket qualification or evidence of absent code. Reconcile saved changes against present owners before dispatch.
2. **One referent graph, qualified identifiers.** Species, individual organism, organs/parts, populations, samples, observations, models, depictions, and HMC releases are distinct referents. Preserve multiple qualified identifiers and source assignments; local indices, matching strings, visual similarity, and shared digests do not establish identity or authority. Keep graph/source/resource lineage through edits, estimates, simulation, LOD, style and derivation.
3. **Invalid and missing observations.** Follow sibling [01-corpus-and-inference.md](01-corpus-and-inference.md). Keep the original invalid observation and its reason intact. An estimate must be an explicitly selected, versioned graph-derived method/query over comparable collected values, recording the actual cohort and query, applicable units, taxon, stage, locality, time, parameters, uncertainty, and lineage. Choose median or another suitable estimator only when justified by the trait/data distribution. There is no universal species median. If comparability or sufficient evidence fails, retain `unresolved` rather than manufacturing a value. Method choice is user-selectable and may be user-defined; native Vibe owns computation/orchestration. Private, proprietary, or classified inputs may be used under the user's policy; record permission-scoped provenance and derived-sharing policy without implying access, disclosure, or classification authority.
4. **State separation.** Label source observation, corrected value, estimate, simulation state, authored artistic choice, and illustrative scenario separately. No synthetic or inferred value may silently overwrite raw collected evidence or appear as a measurement. Record uncertainty and evidence grade at the fact/parameter/relation level.
5. **Human-facing native operations.** A Rust API, MCP tool, headless source, GUI stub, or render receipt alone is not authoring usability. Each selected operation needs an actual reachable native Poet action, inspectable result/error, undo/rebuild, cancellation/bounded-work behaviour, durable save and fresh-process reopen, plus IP-03 C1–C8 contributor/reviewer evidence on an identified project. Do not require JavaScript, Node, or V8 in the native authoring/production path; qualify WASM separately.
6. **Scientific limits are explicit.** Shared numerical mechanisms are not validated biological laws. Record taxon, individual/sample, life stage, environmental and geographic scope, measurement frame/units, model assumptions, calibration set, applicable validity domain, known failure cases and independent reviewer. A visual result, source test, synthetic benchmark, or source file hash cannot claim domain validity or acceptance.
7. **Package completeness stays external to a lane.** Each specimen uses the pinned catalogue/dossier and mandatory claim ledger; all applicable anatomy, graph/resources, states, behaviour/scenario and film/open-world-game/cartoon targets remain explicit. A bounded lane may deliver an accepted subcase but cannot mark the species/model package complete without every applicable catalogue claim, evidence/review, and release gate.

## Packet: vascular and other plants

**Preserve and reuse.** The canonical Core host `ComputationalGeometry.turtle_3d` calls the existing geometry kernel. Poet's native geometry host already admitted it with borrowed-input validation and cumulative work/dispatch bounds; saved 10 host cases, one offscreen GPU/editor case and a native build are retained in [biological-reference execution](../../qa/implementation-2026-10-26/biological-reference/execution.md). Three authored fixtures are explicitly branch-geometry studies, not plant stages or species models. Do not redo turtle admission or call the fixtures growth. Core already exposes `space_colonize_3d`, `tree_skeleton_3d`, and `pipe_model_radii` through `wave27_host.rs`/`wave29_host.rs` and invocation dispatch; these produce graph/radii data, not a connected species mesh or growth grammar. `qualia-sim` exports `MetamerPlantGraph`, `LeafVascularSkeleton`, `FloraBiomechanicsEvaluator`, leaf flutter/aeroelastic and botanical coupling. `qualia-sim/src/lib.rs::tests::test_flora_biophysics_and_ground_cover_pipeline` is an actual in-crate caller of the leaf, metamer and flora APIs; it is source-level test code, not a fresh execution result or a Poet consumer. The public exports are in `crates/qualia-sim/src/lib.rs`; owners are in the source pins below. The saved whitegum dossier and source receipts remain authoritative for that bounded work.

**Assignments.**

- **Plant graph and organs:** Define the sourced taxon/individual and a stable branch/organ graph with root and parent constraints, namespaced identifiers, organ classes, developmental state, coordinate frame and provenance. Integrate existing colonization/reconstruction/radii or authored Vibe graph recipes with existing turtle/section-sweep geometry. Provide separate exact inputs/outputs and graph inspection; never treat colonization iterations as age. Keep measured branches, inferred branches and artistic repair distinguishable.
- **Growth and physiology:** Adapter in the sim/host direction (Core must not depend back on `qualia-sim`). Bind metamer age/thermal time, organ transitions, source-sink, water/leaf response, environment and wind only after selecting sourced, versioned parameters and stable per-organ correspondence. The saved `MetamerPlantGraph::update_thermal_time` and `solve_growth_cycle` are fixed-capacity implementations with example/default assumptions; handle capacity and ordering explicitly. Continue leaf hydraulic/venation and flora-life-form owners without silently applying eucalyptus presets to other taxa.
- **Other flora:** Select a subject whose real morphology requires a distinct form (e.g. grass, fern/frond, vine, geophyte, aquatic thallus, or colony). Reuse shared topology/math but author and review its taxon-specific structure and lifecycle. Do not widen the tree grammar to claim these cases.
- **Native Poet controls:** Integrate graph/organ selection, sourced parameter and method inspection, stage/time/environment selection, controlled regeneration, foliage/material controls, selection/picking, undo and replay into canonical source/session and connected viewport. Extend persistence/HMC only through current owners and after the selected format contracts are reconciled.

**Deliverables and acceptance.** An editable source graph; output geometry with connected topology and organ/branch lineage; source-linked parameter/cohort records; at least one justified plant stage transition retaining correspondence; foliage/reproductive/seasonal cases as applicable to the selected subject; material and wind/contact result where required; native contributor edit → preview → undo/rebuild → HMC save/reopen evidence; reference comparison and qualified plant review. For the whitegum, preserve TREE-01–13 and ECO-061–080 dispositions, current case identifiers, and the three required target profiles. Additional plant forms need a separate dossier row, not substitution into whitegum acceptance.

**Validation and limits.** Bound graph nodes/edges, iterations, scratch, result size, and cancellation before allocation. Test parent validity/acyclicity, units and frames, identifier/lineage persistence, deterministic replay for fixed inputs, stage transitions with source ranges, and refusal on out-of-domain inputs. Compare to measured/curated architecture and traits with uncertainty and domain review. Validate morphology and physiological outputs independently: Shinozaki-style pipe allocation, thermal time, simplified frost/drought thresholds and turgor-driven stiffness are model mechanisms, not species truth or universal growth laws. Do not infer causal ecology from correlations or use inferred missing trait values without the graph-estimate contract above.

**Dependencies.** Source/register qualification and identifiers (IP-00); native format, graph/resource and selected M64/HMC profile (IP-01/02); reachable bounded graph operators and shared authoring state (IP-02/03); actual cooperative Poet proof (IP-03); reviewed master and derivatives (IP-04/05).

**Dependencies.** Source/referent and anatomy mapping (IP-00/01); supported acquisition/volume profile where used (IP-02); connected editable surface and rig/viewport/persistent state (IP-02/03); cooperative review (IP-03); domain-reviewed master and derivative motion (IP-04/05).

## Packet: insects and other invertebrates

**Reuse boundary.** Reuse geometry/topology, skeletal/kinematic constraints, soft-body and optics/volume mechanisms as appropriate. The nine-location review specifically cautions that selected vision/volume and image-to-3D paths may be illustrative (e.g. luminance heightfields) and cannot imply calibrated microscopic or taxon-accurate reconstruction. `qualia-sim` generic `ArticulatedRagdoll`, rigid contacts, hydro, and the Core geometry hosts are caller examples, not insect models. No inspected evidence demonstrates a complete native insect or other invertebrate authoring workflow.

**Assignments.** Select a concrete taxon and environment. Model segmented regions, articulated appendages, attachment/joint constraints, exoskeleton or soft body, and optical structures only where the evidence supports them. Keep larval/nymph/adult or other metamorphic transitions as explicit individual/lineage-linked state changes; represent wing and flight mechanics only when required and calibrated. For aquatic invertebrates, define buoyancy/drag/flow and boundary conditions separately from terrestrial contact. Integrate existing constraints and collision APIs in the sim/host owner, then expose bounded editing and inspection in Poet. Include all required dissimilar parts; no one insect stands for all invertebrates.

**Deliverables and acceptance.** A dossier-backed graph and editable surfaces with segment/appendage identifiers; supported stage-specific anatomy; a selected locomotion/contact or aquatic interaction case; optical/surface response when relevant; source-linked measurements and uncertainty; native source/viewport/save/reopen and contributor/reviewer work; per-target derivation status.

**Validation and limits.** Verify joint ranges/frames, connectivity, mass/scale, collision capacity, transition continuity, no silent truncation, finite/bounded state, and calibrated view/measurement scale. Compare to sourced specimens or validated reference data; domain review each taxon and stage. A generic articulated rig does not establish exoskeleton mechanics, wing aerodynamics, metamorphosis or soft-body physiology. Keep simulated/inferred behaviour distinct from observed behaviour.

**Dependencies.** Subject/reference and scale profile (IP-00/01); calibrated acquisition and representation (IP-02); chosen geometry/optical/simulation operations (IP-02/03); Poet controls and cooperative authoring (IP-03); independent acceptance and derivatives (IP-04/05).

## Packet: funga

**Reuse boundary.** Use existing graph/curve/surface/volume/field and hydrology mechanisms. Core's space-colonization and tree-skeleton routines return graphs; they do not supply fungal topology, hyphal branching biology, anastomosis, fruiting-body architecture, spore dispersal or lifecycle. `qualia-sim` flora models are plant-specific, and should not be relabelled fungal. The nine-location review's selected environmental raster, volume, solver and renderer examples are useful owners, not complete fungal workflows.

**Assignments.** Select a fungus and explicit biological question. Represent hyphae/mycelium, observed branching/anastomosing connections, substrate contact, fruiting/reproductive structures and life-stage/time as distinct graph resources with identifiers and evidence. Use network fields, diffusion or surface growth only where the chosen model has declared boundary conditions and validated equations. Connect fungal structures to host/plant/deadwood/soil referents through qualified relation provenance; measured association, inferred interaction, and hypothesis must remain distinct. Integrate a native inspection/timeline path for network topology and scales.

**Deliverables and acceptance.** Source-linked taxon/individual or colony graph; editable network and reproductive/body surfaces; substrate/environment context; documented temporal state or explicitly scoped static case; queryable hyphal/component IDs, calibrated scale, provenance and uncertainty; actual native authoring and HMC reopen; expert review of structure and the represented relation set.

**Validation and limits.** Preserve topology/branch and sample lineage through remesh/LOD; bound graph density, field resolution, propagation work and scratch; refuse out-of-scope geometry and unknown state. Compare network statistics and structures only to comparable source cohorts, methods, growth stage and observation scale. Do not call a branch mesh a mycelium, an appealing mushroom a complete fungus, or network co-occurrence a proven symbiosis/decomposition mechanism. Never impute a universal fungal parameter from a plant or generic biological cohort.

**Dependencies.** Corpus/right/identity and substrate sources (IP-00/01); graph/volume and field profile (IP-02); bounded domain-specific geometry/simulation and Poet interaction (IP-03); reviewed master and declared use derivatives (IP-04/05).

## Packet: microorganisms and microbial communities

**Reuse boundary.** Prefer existing image/volume acquisition and metadata, vision/segmentation, graph query, typed sampled fields, optics and native renderer. BSA-02/07/08/12 and the nine-location vision/audio/render review expressly separate real acquisition/calibration from a synthetic view or luminance heightfield. Existing geometry is useful for reconstruction only after source scale, frame, segmentation and uncertainty are established. This inspection found no complete native microbial production path; that is an unassessed end-to-end outcome, not proof of absent individual operators.

**Assignments.** Choose one named cell, microbial sample, colony or biofilm and an actual source modality. Preserve original source image/volume, calibration, acquisition metadata, channel identity, voxel spacing, slice/frame registration, quality flags and rights. Declare segmentation or reconstruction method and version, and retain labels, boundaries, components and uncertain regions as source-linked graph resources. Represent internal structures only when the modality/reference supports them. Keep cell vs colony vs community scale, individual/sample vs taxon identity, and observation vs inference distinct. A private/classified source can stay private under user policy; derived access follows the shared provenance rules.

**Deliverables and acceptance.** Source-preserving calibrated dataset reference; editable label/segmentation and component graph; complete measurement frame and uncertainty; inspectable slices/volume and 3D representation that exposes acquisition and rendering method; native query/selection, save/reopen and permission-scoped sharing behavior; domain review for the stated educational/research purpose.

**Validation and limits.** Use known spacing/orientation and phantom or independent annotations where available; verify interpolation, registration, segmentation agreement, boundary cases, label-to-mesh topology, scale bar/units, uncertainty propagation, and preservation of originals. Report modality limits, resolution/aliasing, stain/contrast assumptions and any inferred hidden structure. A MIP is not a true section; a rendering is not a calibrated microscope; attractive internal detail must not be invented. No clinical/diagnostic claim is authorized by visualization or biology acceptance.

**Dependencies.** Rights and modality/source profile (IP-00/01); supported native acquisition, scientific resource and volume display path (IP-02); bounded segmentation/query/authoring/format support (IP-02/03); educator/domain review (IP-04/09 as applicable).

## Packet: habitats, communities and living-system interactions

**Reuse and callers.** Use Q42 graph/identifier/provenance, Vibe native computation, geospatial place/field owners, and existing `qualia-sim` rather than a new ecology engine. Concrete owners inspected include `FloraBiomechanicsEvaluator::step_environment`; `fire_ecology::{evaluate_dead_fine_fuel_moisture,evaluate_fireline_intensity}` with the thin `qualia-mcp` `sim_fire_ecology` wrapper; `hydro` buoyancy/wave/hysteresis paths; `space_weather` solar-terrestrial functions; and botanical/soil/water continuum bridges. MCP's JSON defaults and labels are example transport only, not the canonical native authoring interface or sourced environmental state. The outstanding-work record already notes the fixed placeholder outputs at a Core studio adapter for space-weather; that seam cannot stand in for the sim owner. Existing solver review distinguishes population growth from organism development and notes order-dependence and bounded pair/capacity concerns. Preserve these findings rather than commission new solvers from search results.

**Assignments.** For a real identified place and time window, join individually or cohort-identified organisms and supported habitat features to source-qualified soil, water, terrain, atmosphere, fire, and relevant solar-terrestrial records. Define spatial/temporal scales, units, frames, boundary conditions, missing/invalid observations, valid model periods, and source conflicts. Author typed interactions (e.g. trophic, pollination, host, decomposition, refuge, competition, disturbance/recovery) as qualified relations with source or hypothesis status. Individual state, population/community change, and environmental process must remain separate simulation owners and temporal scales. Let the user select a versioned computation method, cohort/query and/or authored native Vibe method; make parameters and uncertainty inspectable. Keep private inputs private and derived sharing permission-scoped.

**Deliverables and acceptance.** A place/time HMC closure linking organisms, habitat resources, source records, graph relations, methods, results, uncertainty and lineage; explicit observed-vs-reanalysis-vs-forecast-vs-scenario status; bounded reproducible native simulation/query with cancellation and invalidation on source change; Poet inspection of premises and result provenance; actual native save/reopen and contributor/reviewer pass; domain review of each asserted ecological link; downstream case comprehension assessed separately where a civics scenario is in scope.

**Validation and limits.** Check frame/unit/time alignment, source coverage, model validity windows, boundary values, work/memory bounds, resource refusal, cancellation, deterministic replay where promised, and graph invalidation after corrections. Compare simulations to independent observations/reference periods and report sensitivity/uncertainty; do not treat fitted agreement as proof of causal mechanism. Fire and climate outputs need operational model/agency review for any consequential use. The existing `qualia-sim` fire/space/weather/hydro mechanisms are simplified and require domain validation before qualifying ecological or safety decisions. Correlation, co-occurrence, fixed adapter outputs, a token/graph relation, or visual change does not prove causal biological effect.

**Dependencies.** Source corpus/graph-derived estimation, privacy and identifiers (IP-00); real place/data profile and native resource mapping (IP-01/02/06); selected Vibe and sim owners integrated with correct dependency direction and actual Poet workflows (IP-02/03); ecological review and assessed use/derivative (IP-04/05/09).
