## Mandatory per-model completion claims

When creating, changing, reviewing or declaring complete any model/species/thing
embodiment, follow [model-completion-claims.md](docs/planning/tech/assets/model-completion-claims.md)
and the [versioned production template](docs/planning/tech/assets/model-production-template/README.md).

- Maintain a dossier and claim ledger pinned to the catalogue version/digest.
- Make an explicit applicability/status/result/evidence claim for every catalogue
  deliverable and every required expanded part, profile, variant, clip,
  transition, behaviour and scenario. Add subject-specific obligations; do not
  reduce scope to available tools or selected demo actions.
- Film, open-world game and cartoon versions are required for complete packages.
  Keep style, LOD, biological state, simulation fidelity and delivery target distinct.
- Record missing/partial/blocked/failed/unassessed work. N/A requires a documented
  applicable rule or authorized decision, never a self-awarded waiver for an
  unsupported feature. No row may silently disappear.
- Bind evidence to actual source/output/package/dependency identifiers. Distinguish
  source presence, executed verification and human acceptance. Do not invent a
  measurement, tool result, signature, human review or completion-gate pass.
- Keep graphs/resources in the complete HMC embodiment; editable sources and
  provenance are required. Screenshots, meshes with labels or animation alone
  do not establish a complete species/thing.
- A bounded task may finish within its declared scope, but the final response
  must state the model/package remains incomplete and identify remaining IDs
  when applicable. Do not call the complete model/package done until every
  applicable deliverable/case and required acceptance has passed.
- A model completion report must link the full ledger and inventories, state
  all three target statuses, identify artifact versions, give gate/evidence and
  review references, and disclose all remaining open IDs.

These are repository instructions, not an installed technical gate. The native
validation/review gate remains implementation work; never claim it is enforced
by CI/runtime until that capability has been implemented and qualified.

Specification/documentation changes alone do not require model claims or imply
any model's completion. Do not run unrequested builds, tests, exports, paid
provider calls, commits, pushes or publication. Read-only inspection and document
consistency checks are appropriate for specification tasks.
